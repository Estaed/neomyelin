# Personality

Active patterns appear below as single lines after at least three days of verified quotes.
Evidence, sleeping patterns, and veto reasons stay in `.brain/patterns.md`.
To veto one, run
`python .brain/scripts/patterns.py veto "<pattern title>"`. Its evidence remains marked
vetoed, so the same line is not proposed again. You may add your own lines; the machine
preserves them.
