# Goals

The map of what you are working toward: school, work, competitions and personal aims. For each
one: what it is, who is involved, where its notes live and in what order you take them on.
Status, due dates and the next step are not written here; they live in the matching task
(`tasks/`), and the map links to it. A line that will go stale belongs in a task or a receipt.

Example sub-folder: `Yearly Plans/`
